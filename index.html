Frequently Asked Questions

How does ScholarPulse prevent AI hallucinations?

We use Claude 3.5's state-of-the-art reasoning APIs coupled with vector embeddings of your uploaded PDFs. The model is strictly instructed to only answer using provided document fragments and must quote page/line coordinates. If a claim cannot be verified in the corpus, it explicitly flags it.

Is this tool free for university students and academics?

Yes. During our academic pilot program in 2026, university students, master's/PhD candidates, and faculty can access ScholarPulse for free by applying with their institutional email address.

Are my unpublished research drafts safe?

Yes. All document processing utilizes enterprise Anthropic API connections with zero data retention (ZDR). Your private manuscripts and theses are never used to train foundation models.
